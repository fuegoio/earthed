---
title: Entries
description: Read, star, search, and manage articles from your feeds.
---

An **entry** is a single article fetched from a feed. Each entry has a read status, a starred flag, and sanitized HTML content. Entries appear in your timeline newest-first — this includes articles shared by people you follow, which surface alongside entries from your own subscriptions.

## Read statuses

Every entry is in one of three states:

| Status    | What it means                                              |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `unread`  | New — you haven't opened it yet. Shows a dot in the timeline. |
| `read`    | You've opened it. Hidden from the **Unread** view.        |
| `removed` | Hidden from the normal views without being marked read.   |

New entries start as `unread`. Opening an article marks it `read` automatically. You can mark a read entry unread again by clicking its dot — useful when you want to come back to something.

## Read an entry

There are two ways to read:

- **Open the original** — click the entry's title to open the article on its source site in a new tab. Sunred marks it read.
- **Read in Sunred** — click the card body to open the in-app reader, which shows the title, author, date, tags, and a summary. From there, open the full article in a new tab with one click.

The in-app reader doesn't render the full article body, because most feeds only carry a summary. The original is always one click away.

## Star entries

Star an entry to bookmark it — click the star icon on the card or in the reader header. Starred entries live under **Starred** in the sidebar, whatever their read status. Click the star again to unstar.

## Share entries

Click the share icon on a card to share the article with people who follow you. Their shared articles appear in your **Unread** and **All** views alongside your own subscriptions. See [Social](/social) for the full picture.

## Search

The search box at the top of the sidebar runs a full-text query across every entry's title and content. Type a query and press Enter; results appear in the timeline like any other view. Combine it with read status and starred filters through the [Entries API](/api-reference?tag=Entries) for more targeted queries.

## Mark all as read

To clear a whole feed at once, open the feed's page and click **Mark all as read**. The unread count drops to zero and those entries leave the **Unread** view.

## Programmatic access

List, filter, update, and star entries through the [Entries API](/api-reference?tag=Entries) — filter by feed, folder, status, starred, or a search query, and paginate with `limit` and `offset`.
