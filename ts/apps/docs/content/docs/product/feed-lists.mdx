---
title: Feed lists
description: Curate collections of feeds, share them, and follow lists from others.
---

A **feed list** is a curated, shareable collection of feeds — a reading list for a topic. A list stores feed URLs and titles, and it's independent of your subscriptions: you can share a list without subscribing to its feeds, and following someone else's list doesn't subscribe you to anything until you import it.

Lists live under **Feed lists** in the sidebar.

## Create a list

Go to **Feed lists → New list**. Give it a title, an optional description, and choose whether it's **public** (discoverable and followable by others) or **private** (only you can see it). You can change all of this later from the list's page.

## Add feeds to a list

On a list you own, add feeds in two ways:

- **From your subscriptions** — search your feeds and click to add them.
- **By URL** — paste any feed or website URL below, with an optional title.

Adding the same URL twice is a no-op. Remove a feed from a list with the trash icon next to it — this never affects your subscriptions.

## Follow a list

Open any public list and click **Follow** to bookmark it. Followed lists appear under **Lists you follow** on the Feed lists page, and you can unfollow from the same place.

You can't follow your own list or a private list.

## Discover lists

Click **Discover** on the Feed lists page to browse public lists others have shared. Follow any that look interesting.

## Import a list's feeds

Following a list keeps it bookmarked but doesn't subscribe you to its feeds. To subscribe to every feed in a list at once, open it and click **Import all feeds**.

Import is idempotent — feeds you already subscribe to are skipped, so it's safe to run more than once. You'll see how many were imported, how many skipped, and how many failed.

## Share a list

On a public list you own, click **Share** to copy a link to the list. Anyone who opens it can follow it or import its feeds (as long as they're on the same instance).

## Edit or delete a list

Only the owner can edit or delete a list. From the list's page, **Edit** changes the title, description, and visibility; **Delete** removes the list and its feeds. Deleting a list never affects anyone's subscriptions — including yours.

## Programmatic access

Create, update, follow, and import lists through the [Feed lists API](/api-reference?tag=Feed%20lists).
