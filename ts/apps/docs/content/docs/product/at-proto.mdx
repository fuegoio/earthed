---
title: Identity & sync
description: How Earthed identity works with AT Proto, and how your data stays in sync.
---

Earthed uses the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) for identity. Your Earthed
account is your AT Proto account — the same one you use on Bluesky or any
other AT Proto app. There is no separate Earthed password.

## Logging in

Sign in with your Bluesky handle (for example `alice.bsky.social`). Earthed
sends you to Bluesky to approve the login, then brings you back signed in.
There's no separate Earthed password — you use the same account you already
have in the Bluesky app.

You can log into any Earthed instance with your handle — you are not tied to a
single server. If your friend runs an Earthed instance and you have a Bluesky
account, you can log into their instance directly. Some instances also offer
to create a new account directly from the login page.

## Where your data lives

Your social data lives on your PDS as public records, not locked inside
Earthed:

- Who you **follow**
- Articles you **share**
- Feeds you **subscribe to**
- Your **feed lists**
- Your Earthed **profile** (handle, bio)

Earthed keeps a local **cache** of these records so the app stays fast, but
your PDS is the source of truth. When you follow, share, or subscribe, Earthed
writes the record to your PDS, and other Earthed users see it through the
relay.

## Syncing to a new instance

The first time you log into an Earthed instance, it pulls your existing
`io.earthed.*` records from your PDS into its local cache so your follows,
shares, and feed subscriptions are all there immediately. After that it stays
in sync via the relay.

State that is not part of your AT Proto records — which entries you've read,
your folders, your per-instance settings — stays on the instance where you
created it. Switching instances means your social graph comes with you (it's
on your PDS), but your local reading state does not.

## Portability

Because your identity and social graph live on your PDS, you're not locked
into Earthed. Another AT Proto-compatible RSS reader can read the same
records, and you can move your PDS without losing your Earthed social data.
