title: Social
description: Follow people, share articles, and see what others are reading.
---

Sunred is social. Because identity runs on the [AT Protocol](/at-proto), you can follow other Sunred users and share articles with them — the same social graph you already have on Bluesky.

## Follow people

Every Sunred user has a profile at `/users/{handle}`. Open one and click **Follow** to follow them. You can follow anyone on your instance; on federated setups you can follow people across instances too.

The people you follow are part of your AT Proto social graph, so they travel with you when you sign into another instance. See [Identity & sync](/at-proto) for how that works.

## Share articles

Click the **share** icon on any entry card to post it to your social timeline. The people who follow you will see it in their **Social** view, with your name attached.

Sharing an article doesn't change its read status or starring — it's an independent action. You can share something you've already read, or star something you've shared.

## The Social view

**Social** in the sidebar shows a timeline of articles shared by the people you follow, newest first. Each card shows who shared it and when. Click the sharer's name to visit their profile, where you can follow or unfollow them.

If you don't follow anyone yet, the view prompts you to follow people first. Once you do, their shares start appearing here automatically.

## Your profile

Under **Settings → Profile**, set the display name and email other Sunred users see on your profile. Your handle comes from your AT Proto account and can't be changed here.

## Programmatic access

Share articles, list who you follow, and read the social timeline through the [Social API](/api-reference?tag=Social).
